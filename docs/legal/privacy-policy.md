# MyCatCat Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

This is a draft for product testing and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before public launch.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how MyCatCat ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the MyCatCat application and related services (the "Service").

## 2. Data We Collect

Depending on features you enable, we may process:

- Account and profile information (for example, account identifiers, contact details).
- Configuration data (model/provider settings, language, feature toggles).
- Interaction data (chat messages, prompts, tool calls, action approvals, diagnostics/logs).
- Device and runtime metadata (app version, OS, timestamps, crash/error logs).
- Permissioned local context (for example screenshots, selected files, clipboard, microphone input), only when you explicitly grant permission and trigger relevant actions.
- Third-party integration data from connected services (for example OAuth tokens, channel metadata), subject to your configuration.

## 3. How We Use Data

We use data to:

- provide and operate the Service,
- execute approved agent actions,
- improve reliability, security, and performance,
- troubleshoot support issues,
- and comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal Bases

Where required by law, we rely on one or more legal bases, such as contract performance, legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligations.

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

We may share data with:

- model and infrastructure providers required to deliver requested features,
- service providers that process data under our instructions,
- and competent authorities when required by law.
  We do not sell personal data.

## 6. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
You can delete local app data by uninstalling and removing related local state directories.

## 7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.
However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
You are responsible for protecting your own device and credentials.

## 8. International Transfers

If data is processed outside your jurisdiction, we use appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent.
Add your data-rights request channel before production release.

## 10. Children

The Service is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law.
Do not use the Service if you are not legally permitted to do so.

## 11. Third-Party Services

Third-party integrations are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.
We are not responsible for third-party data practices.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The updated version becomes effective when published with a new "Last Updated" date.

## 13. Contact

For privacy inquiries, add a support/privacy contact channel before production release.
